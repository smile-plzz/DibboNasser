Background

About Dibbo

Dibbo Nasser is a Dhaka-based guitarist and composer, lead guitarist of Chirkutt, the internationally performing Bangladeshi folk-rock band, and founder of the progressive rock band Fantasy Realm, established in 2019.

In 2025 he contributed to Chirkutt's fourth studio album, Bhalobasha Shomogro, and released Fantasy Realm's studio album, 21g.

His session guitar work includes projects with the late Shafin Ahmed (Miles), Sunidhi Nayak, Pritom Hasan, Rafa, Arekta Rock Band, and Shishir Ahmed. He has also contributed to music for films, television dramas, television commercials, jingles, voice-over productions, and collaborative arrangements.

His professional experience includes time at Radio Today 89.6 FM and work associated with Niketon, which shaped a working habit of serving the needs of a piece, a brand, or a collaborator before his own instincts.
